## added

- plays hap, notchlc, prores, h.264, hevc and vp9, and numbered stills as one clip
- steps one true frame at a time; the timebar shows the frame under the pointer
- zoom to the pixel with its values, a navigator, guides, mirror, flip and rotate
- metadata read from the file, with what it does not say labeled
- loop analysis shows how a clip's end meets its start
- a browser with an address bar, back and forward, list or gallery, and scrubbing previews
- a folder, or several picked files, play one after another
- screenshots with a caption, and markup on the frame
- settings, help and about, from the gear and ? on the strip
- a newer release shows an update button; one press puts it in place
